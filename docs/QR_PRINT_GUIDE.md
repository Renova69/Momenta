# QR Canvas & Signage Printing Guide

This guide provides print specifications, dimensions, and best practices for creating A2/A3 welcome easels, table cards, and bar signage for weddings and events.

---

## 1. Print Format Standards

| Format | Dimensions (mm) | Dimensions (Inches) | Recommended Placement | Optimal Scan Distance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A2 Poster / Canvas** | 420 x 594 mm | 16.5 x 23.4 in | Вход на ресторанта, статив за Welcome Drink | 1.0 m – 3.0 m (3–10 ft) |
| **A3 Poster / Board** | 297 x 420 mm | 11.7 x 16.5 in | Кът за снимки, DJ пулт, Бар | 0.5 m – 1.5 m (2–5 ft) |
| **Table Tent Card (A5 / A6 Сгъната)** | 148 x 210 mm | 5.8 x 8.3 in | Маси на гостите, коктейлни маси | 0.3 m – 0.8 m (1–2.5 ft) |

---

## 2. QR Code Sizing Rules for Large Canvases

To guarantee that guest phone cameras (iOS & Android) instantly detect and focus on the QR code in ambient or dim wedding lighting:

* **Easel Canvas (A2)**: QR code size must be at least **12 cm x 12 cm (4.7 x 4.7 in)**.
* **Bar / Guestbook Board (A3)**: QR code size must be at least **8 cm x 8 cm (3.2 x 3.2 in)**.
* **Table Tent Card**: QR code size must be at least **4 cm x 4 cm (1.6 x 1.6 in)**.

---

## 3. Visual & Lighting Best Practices

1. **High Contrast is Critical**:
   * Always ensure high contrast between the QR code and the background (e.g. Deep Charcoal/Gold code on Cream/White cardstock).
   * Avoid printing dark QR codes on dark textured canvas or metallic foil where glare can obscure the pattern.
2. **Matte Finish over Glossy**:
   * For A2/A3 foam boards or canvases, always choose **Matte** or **Satin** finish.
   * Glossy surfaces create harsh reflections from venue fairy lights and chandeliers, interfering with phone lenses.
3. **Short & Clean URL**:
   * Use clean slug URLs (e.g., `https://wedmoments.bg/e/monika-and-alexander-2026`) with high error correction (Level M or Q).
